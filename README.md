 AI-Powered-Hostel-Food-Quality-Monitoring-System
Hostel Food Insight AI is a smart hostel food quality morning system that enables students to rate meals based on taste, quality, hygiene and proper  menu. The platform includes a complaint management system, automatically records students details and meal timings(Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner), and generates alerts for hostel management when food
quality falls below a defined threshold. The system helps improve food services through real-time feedback and data-driven insights.
Key Features
🍽️ Student Food Feedback & Ratings
⭐ Multi-Criteria Rating (Taste, Quality, Hygiene)
📝 Complaint Submission System
🚨 Automatic Low Food Quality Alerts
👨‍🎓 Student Information Recording (Name & Registration Number)
🕒 Meal-wise Feedback (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).
